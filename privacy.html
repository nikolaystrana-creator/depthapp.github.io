Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 10, 2026

Depth is provided by Oleg Zharin, an independent developer based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates ("we", "us"). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Depth app, visit this website, or contact us. Contact: blastsellers@gmail.com.

The purchase-related processing below applies to app versions that include subscriptions and RevenueCat. Earlier versions without this integration do not send subscription information to RevenueCat.

1. Information on your device

No Depth account is required. The app keeps preferences, onboarding status, favorite card identifiers, progress, and daily selections on your device. Depth does not record your spoken conversations or ask you to submit answers to its conversation cards. These conversations and answers are not sent to us or RevenueCat.

Optional reminders use local notifications with your permission; you can disable them in iOS Settings. If you choose to share a card using the iOS share sheet, the receiving app handles the content under its own privacy practices. Device backups may include local app data according to your Apple settings.

2. Subscriptions and RevenueCat

In versions with subscriptions, we use Apple In-App Purchase and RevenueCat to display offers, verify purchases, provide and restore Premium access, and understand subscription performance. RevenueCat may be contacted when you open the app or view offers, even if you do not purchase.

RevenueCat processes a randomly generated app user identifier; purchase and receipt or transaction information (such as product identifiers, purchase and expiry dates, renewal and refund status); subscription access status; and technical information such as app version, device platform, operating system, store country, and currency. This identifier is pseudonymous: it distinguishes an app customer but is not a registration with your name or email. We do not supply RevenueCat with your conversation answers, contact list, email address, or advertising identifier.

We use this information to operate subscriptions, resolve access problems, prevent purchase fraud, and review aggregate subscription and revenue reports. Apple processes payment details; we and RevenueCat do not receive your full payment-card number. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. Website and support

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting it sends ordinary web-request information, including your IP address and browser information, to the hosting provider. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which also receives the network information needed to deliver those fonts. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and Google Privacy Policy. We do not add advertising trackers or analytics scripts to this website.

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. An app feedback message may include the app version and device or operating-system details shown in the draft. We use this information to answer your request and investigate problems. Please do not send passwords, full payment-card details, or sensitive conversation content.

4. Purposes and legal bases

Where applicable data-protection law requires a legal basis, we process information as necessary to provide the subscription or service you request, for our legitimate interests in operating and securing the app and understanding subscription performance, and to meet legal obligations. Where processing requires consent, you may withdraw it; for example, notification permission can be withdrawn in device settings. Withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing.

5. Sharing and international processing

Information is handled by the providers described above as needed to deliver their services. RevenueCat processes subscription information on our behalf. Apple and other providers may also process information for their own purposes as described in their policies. We may disclose relevant information when required by law or necessary to protect legal rights. We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-app advertising tracking.

We operate from the United Arab Emirates, and providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, transfers must rely on safeguards permitted by applicable law, such as contractual safeguards. You can contact us for information about the arrangements relevant to your data.

6. Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you reset it or delete the app, subject to any device backups. Subscription records are retained as needed to provide and restore access, address billing questions, prevent fraud, and comply with applicable legal obligations. Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve requests and related issues.

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription or automatically delete records held by Apple or RevenueCat. For requests concerning data we control, email us. We may need a purchase reference or other proportionate information to locate your records and verify your request. Some records may need to be retained for legal reasons.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, and complain to your data-protection authority. Contact blastsellers@gmail.com to exercise these rights. For data Apple controls, you can also use Apple's privacy tools. Manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account; see our Terms of Use.

8. Changes and contact

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date above. Where required, we will provide additional notice of material changes. Questions about privacy can be sent to Oleg Zharin at blastsellers@gmail.com.
